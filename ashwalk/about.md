A side-scrolling fog platformer. Your Rare Friend walks the woods, climbs ladders, lights lanterns, and solves a puzzle to leave each fog. A death leaves a pile of bones.

## How to play

- **Move** with A and D or the arrows. **Jump** with W, Up or Space. **S** drops through a thin plank. **E** pulls a rope, lights a bell, buys a lantern or climbs a ladder. On a phone, the buttons sit at the bottom.
- **Stage coins** are picked up inside a fog and spent there: a lantern is 1, the mark is 2 (a death then keeps your coins), and the flashlight is 5. The shore starts you with 2, beating a fog adds 5, and up to 12 come with you. They are not $RAREFRIENDS.
- **Three lives** to a board. A death drops half the stage coins you picked up, unless you paid for the mark.
- **Fogs open in order.** Beat a fog before you can open the next, or buy the fog pass to open every fog at once. The hallow, mirror, tunnel, eve, hoist and stack open on their dates.
