Your Rare Friend washes ashore on a small island. Keep it alive, grow the island into a home, and take your chances at sea.

## How to play

1. **Survive.** Hunger, energy and mood drain in real time. Eat what you grow or catch, and sleep at home to refill energy.
2. **Gather and grow.** Chop palms, quarry stone and sea glass, farm kelp, carrots, berries and pumpkins, and fish in a timing game. Every action earns SHELL, the island coin.
3. **Build.** Upgrade your home from a beach tent to a Sea Castle. Better homes mean better sleep and a safer vault.
4. **Take risks.** Level up with ascension rolls, forge your gear from +1 to +10, dive into the Abyss and cash out before it bites, dig the Tide Mines and spin the Tide Wheel. Every chance is shown before you play.
5. **Set sail.** Send your Friend on timed voyages and see what the sea gives back: materials, pearls, relics or nothing at all.
6. **Explore.** Unlock new regions one by one, from Rock Cove to Kraken Reef, and follow the story chapter by chapter.
7. **Go again.** Rebirth at level 30 for permanent bonuses. The island never ends.

Your Friend is drawn from its own on-chain art, and its family gives it a unique trait. Your island is saved on the game server, so it follows your Friend to any device.
