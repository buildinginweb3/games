Your Friend enters a 50-player battle royale on an island that shrinks until one remains. Choose where to drop, pick a three-item loadout and start with a **Fight, Hide or Loot** strategy.

### Three entry levels

Choose a stake of **100, 1,000 or 10,000 $RAREFRIENDS**. The standard loadout—Shield, Medkit and Armor plate—is included. Some other three-item combinations add a surcharge. Every entry also includes a **15 $RAREFRIENDS settlement fee**, plus the Dice fee and network gas in ETH. The game shows the complete cost before you enter.

### The round

Rare Friends drop onto the island, search crates for gear and move toward safety as the storm closes in. Use active items yourself or let your Friend trigger them automatically. You can change strategy up to three times during a round, with a short cooldown between changes.

### Prizes and settlement

The top ten finishers receive prizes based on the selected stake and final placement. Loadout surcharges and fees do not increase the prize. After settlement, winnings go to your Friend’s wallet and the completed round can be replayed from its published record.

Rare Royale is based on DEDQ3E’s Vibeathon submission and rebuilt for the Rare Friends platform.
