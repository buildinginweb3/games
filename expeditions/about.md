Send your Rare Friend on expeditions through the Forest, the Crystal Cave and the Sunken Ruins. Every run ends at a chest with one find.

## How to play

1. **Get a pass.** An Expedition Pass costs 1,000 $RAREFRIENDS and stays with your Friend until it sets out.
2. **Set out.** The pass decides the find when your Friend leaves; play the run and open the chest at the end. Finds range from Common to Mythic.
3. **Keep or sell.** Kept finds boost your Friend's XP on every expedition (Rare and above add a glow and a keepsake). Sell a find any time for its fixed value: 400 to 10,000 $RAREFRIENDS.
4. **Outfit.** The Outfitter sells gear (Trail Backpack, Spring Boots, Cave Lantern, Ruins Map), rations and cosmetics, each at its own price, paid together in one payment.

Weekly leaderboards rank the best runs in each place, and the showcase ranks Friends by the value of the finds they keep. Progress, finds and outfits stay with your Friend.
