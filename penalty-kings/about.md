Early access preview of Penalty Kings by **@NooNe0x** · [@phon_ro](https://x.com/phon_ro).

Buy a two-ball pack for **2 USDG**, choose a ball, and take your penalty. Packs are available in quantities of 1, 2, 5 or 10. Each kick consumes one ball. Winning kicks pay **2–64 USDG**, depending on the ball and result.

Balls and winnings belong to your selected Friend’s wallet. Your connected wallet submits transactions and pays gas; packs are paid in USDG to the Rare Friends platform treasury. Better-generation Friends have a higher rating; aiming controls the animation, while the platform contracts determine paid results from Dice randomness and settle each kick automatically once it is drawn.

**Practice** is free. **Daily Challenge** awards points and saves progress with your Friend. Neither mode awards money.
