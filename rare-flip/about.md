**Bet 1,000–25,000 $RAREFRIENDS** per flip, in steps of 1,000. Pick heads or tails; each flip is a single 50/50 draw settled on chain. A win pays **1.88×–1.98×** your bet, depending on your Friend’s generation. A loss pays nothing.

### Paying for a flip

One transaction pays the bet in $RAREFRIENDS and a small ETH randomness fee. The game shows both before you confirm. Only Friends you own can play.

### Winnings

Wins are paid to your Friend’s wallet when the flip settles, usually within seconds. There is nothing to claim. If a flip’s randomness has not arrived after an hour, the bet is refunded to your Friend’s wallet.

### FLIP and cosmetics

Every 1,000 $RAREFRIENDS bet earns 1 FLIP, win or lose. Spend FLIP in the in-game shop on room decorations, costumes, effects and pets. FLIP cannot be redeemed for $RAREFRIENDS, cosmetics do not change the odds, and both reset when the game reloads.
