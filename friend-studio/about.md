Turn your Rare Friend into a one-of-a-kind PFP. Your Friend's canonical on-chain sprite stays exactly as it is while you layer traits around and over it.

## How to play

1. **Pick.** Your Friend's family and generation decide its perks: two family-exclusive traits for every family, and a gold background for generation 1.
2. **Mix.** Tap any of 100 backgrounds, headwear, eyewear, props and effects to preview them on your Friend. Traits you don't own show a PREVIEW stamp. Randomize, Undo, Reset and Save are one tap each.
3. **Unlock.** Each paid trait has its own $RAREFRIENDS price: 1,000 for Commons, 12,500 for Rares and 123,000 for animated Legendaries. A whole look unlocks in one payment. Rare traits have 500 editions each and Legendary traits 25 each, numbered in the order they're unlocked.
4. **Export.** Save a PNG, or a looping GIF for animated looks, to your device.

Unlocked traits and saved looks stay with your Friend and can be reused in any look. Keys: `R` randomize, `Z` undo, `S` save, `E` export, `1`–`5` trait tabs.
