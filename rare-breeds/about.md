**Garden costs 5,000 $RAREFRIENDS** per breed; **Grove costs 20,000**. Each breed produces one Common, Spotted, Mutant or Prismatic offspring. Rarity odds depend on your selected Friend’s generation and hatchery.

### Hearts

Kept offspring earn Hearts: Common earns 1 per day, Spotted 2, Mutant 4 and Prismatic 10, capped at **10 Hearts per Friend per day**. Eight cosmetics cost 20–250 Hearts. Hearts cannot be redeemed for $RAREFRIENDS and do not affect rarity odds or payouts.

### Trade-ins

The Sanctuary pays **0.5×** the breeding price for Common, **1×** for Spotted, **1.5×** for Mutant and **6×** for Prismatic. Trading in consumes the offspring and pays your connected wallet. The Store tab lists all trade-in values and cosmetic prices.

### Slingshot

Launch an offspring for a payout of **0×, 0.5×, 1×, 2×, 5× or 10×** its launch stake. Launching consumes it, including on a zero payout. The stake is the lower of its trade-in value and breeding price. Each launch costs an ETH randomness fee plus network gas; winnings go to the wallet that launched.

### Odds and expected returns

Mutant odds are **12.5%** and Prismatic odds are **2.5%** in both hatcheries. Common and Spotted chances depend on your Friend’s generation. Garden’s expected trade-in return ranges from 92% at generation 1 to 87% at generation 6; Grove’s ranges from 94.25% to 89.25%. Slingshot has a 90% expected return and the same 10× cap for everyone. These are long-run averages, not guaranteed results.
