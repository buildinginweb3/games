### Memories and friendship

Choose a Rare Friend and complete three activities, then save a memory for **+8 friendship**. One memory unlocks a **Handmade sprout**; three unlock a **Memory frame**, and seven unlock a **Patchwork cushion**.

### Activities and traits

Needs, wishes, favourite activities, favourite foods, birthdays, habits and reactions vary by Friend. Reading, dancing, playing, cooking a pantry meal and visiting a neighbour can count toward memories without a purchase.

### Home and wardrobe

Furniture can be placed, moved or stored, and clothes can be equipped. Sprouts and memory frames can sit on supported tables or on the floor. Home and progress data are stored for the selected Friend.

Optional furniture, outfits and treats use **$RAREFRIENDS from your connected owner wallet**. A **Gift Box costs 100 $RAREFRIENDS** and contains one random keepsake with a **0.35–5 $RAREFRIENDS** trade-in value. Trade-ins pay your Friend’s wallet. The Store tab lists exact prices and odds. Paid actions ask for confirmation before your wallet opens.
